## Scope and sources

CBSE 2026–27 standard syllabus; Class IX uses the revised standard outlines. SST in Classes XI–XII is a navigation grouping of History, Geography (including practical units), Political Science/Theory and Economics. Advanced-level variants and other humanities electives are not included. Formative enrichment is separately labeled in the catalog. Consult the official syllabus for assessment exclusions and exact requirements.

[Official curriculum index](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/curriculum_2027.html). Each chapter links its source PDF. Source URL/checksum metadata is included in `content/curriculum_sources.json`; CBSE PDFs are not redistributed.

The TinyLM remains unchanged: 40 original training completions for Equilibrium and Quadratic Equations. Additional foundation notes and generic replies are authored content, not new neural capabilities.

## Extending coverage

Add original or appropriately licensed explanations and independently checked questions. Preserve deployed IDs, add meaningful worked examples and misconception coverage, and mark readiness accurately. A catalog entry must not be promoted as a fully taught chapter solely because it has one question.
